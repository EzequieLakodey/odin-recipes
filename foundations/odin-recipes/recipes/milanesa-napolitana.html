Milanesa a la Napolitana

Description

A classic Argentine dish: a breaded and fried beef cutlet topped with tomato sauce, ham and melted mozzarella.

It is usually served with french fries or mashed potatoes and is a staple of family lunches.

Ingredients

  • 4 thin beef cutlets (peceto or nalga)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup of breadcrumbs
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Chopped parsley
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 cup of tomato sauce
  • 4 slices of ham
  • 4 slices of mozzarella
  • Dried oregano

Steps

  1. Beat the eggs with the garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  2. Dip each cutlet in the egg mixture.
  3. Coat both sides with breadcrumbs, pressing firmly.
  4. Fry the cutlets in hot oil until golden on both sides.
  5. Place them on a baking tray and top each with tomato sauce, a slice of ham and a slice of mozzarella.
  6. Sprinkle with oregano and bake at 200 °C for 10 minutes, until the cheese melts.
  7. Serve hot with french fries or mashed potatoes.
