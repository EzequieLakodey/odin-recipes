Empanadas de Carne

Description

Baked pastries filled with seasoned ground beef, onion, egg and olives. A must at any Argentine gathering.

Each province has its own version, but the beef empanada is loved everywhere in the country.

Ingredients

  • 12 empanada pastry discs
  • 500 g of ground beef
  • 3 onions, chopped
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • Green olives, sliced
  • 1 tsp of ground cumin
  • 1 tsp of sweet paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil
  • 1 beaten egg (to brush the tops)

Steps

  1. Sauté the onions in oil until soft and translucent.
  2. Add the beef, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until browned.
  3. Let the filling cool, then mix in the chopped eggs and olives.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each pastry disc.
  5. Fold the discs in half and seal the edges with the "repulgue" (braided edge).
  6. Brush the empanadas with the beaten egg.
  7. Bake at 200 °C for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden.
